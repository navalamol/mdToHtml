UX Changes by Domain#

Shell / navigation#

  • New routes:
    • /vendor-portal/onboarding/new (VendorOnboardingWizard, requires login).
    • /supplier-registration (SupplierRegistration, public with no login).
    • /vendor-scoring now redirects to /vendor-portal.
  • Vendor-portal sub-tabs are now List, Syndigo Vendor Network, Vendor Insights (new), Messages, Onboarding and Governance. Compliance, Compare and Scoring were removed; their old ?tab= values redirect to insights.
  • ContextSidebar:
    • Sub-tabs are now <a href> links built by getSubTabHref.
    • The active tab syncs from the route or from ?tab=.
    • The sidebar is hidden on Vendor Central sub-routes and on the vendor-network, insights, messaging, onboarding and governance tabs.
  • AppBreadcrumbs (new):
    • Mounted globally in DashboardLayout.
    • Built automatically from the route: Dashboard › group › module › sub-tab › detail.
    • The detail label comes from breadcrumb-store, which product and vendor detail pages set.
  • Right rail:
    • DashboardLayout shows RightRailTabs when a page has registered audit items through audit-rail-store; otherwise it shows AgentSidebar.
    • RightRailTabs has two tabs: "AI Agents" (280px) and an audit tab (340px, with a count). Both collapse to an icon strip.

AI layer (Synapse)#

  • Launcher: The floating chat bubbles (ChatDock) are removed. AskPxeAiLauncher becomes a floating "Synapse AI" button with an unread count.
  • SynapseSidebar (new):
    • Docked on the right and pushes page content aside: 360px at sm, 400px at lg, fullscreen on mobile.
    • Ask Agents tab: suggested prompts for the current screen, plus a list of agents.
    • Chats tab:
      • Search across agents, people and message text.
      • Filter chips: All, Unread, Agents, Mentions.
      • Sections: Mentions, Messages, Active, Past conversations, and "Start a new conversation" with coworkers.
      • A Clear chats action.
  • ChatWindow:
    • New variant="panel".
    • Typing @ opens autocomplete for people and agents. Mentions are highlighted, and each mentioned coworker gets a DM ping and a toast.
  • Demo data: Agent chats, coworker threads and mentions are seeded once per user (ai-chat-seed), guarded by a localStorage flag.

Shared#

  • AuditTrail:
    • Item shape: AuditItem {id, type: Create|Update|Delete, summary, actor, actorType: User|System, source, timestamp, reason?, changes[{attribute, previous, next}]}.
    • Shown as a vertical timeline. Entries expand to a metadata grid and a previous → next diff.
    • Search, a User/System toggle, and chips for each change type with counts.
    • Export to XLSX (two sheets) or PDF.
  • Audit panels: StackedAuditPanel and AuditHistoryPanel were added, but nothing mounts StackedAuditPanel yet.
  • MaskedInput / pii-mask:
    • Shows the masked value at rest and the raw value on focus, with an eye toggle.
    • Supported kinds: name, email, phone, account, taxid, address and generic.

Vendor list / portal#

  • The list reads comma-separated ?status= and ?q= from the URL to prefill its filters.
  • The card button "View" becomes "View Details", and the overflow menu (more-options button) is removed.
  • "Add vendor" now goes to /vendor-portal/onboarding/new.

Vendor Insights (new; replaces Comparison, Compliance and Scorecard)#

  • Layout:
    • Four KPI cards: Avg Performance, Avg Compliance %, Needing Attention, Certs Expiring.
    • A main column, plus a 340px AIInsightsPanel on the right.
  • Vendor table:
    • Checkbox selection, sortable numeric columns, Risk and Trend columns, expandable rows.
    • Filters: category, risk and period.
  • Tabs:
    • Overview.
    • Performance: a trend chart with a metric picker.
    • Compliance.
    • Compare: enabled when 2–4 vendors are selected, and switches to the tab automatically.
  • AI insight shape: {type, vendorId?, impact, headline, explanation, recommendation} with Accept and Dismiss actions.
  • VendorScoreBadge: colour bands at 90, 80 and 70, with a breakdown tooltip.

Vendor Profile#

  • Layout:
    • The tabs become one scrolling page with sections: details, banking, dnb, documents, additional and timeline.
    • A sticky VendorSectionNav tracks the current section and shows a progress line.
  • Header: a single Edit/Save/Cancel, View Portal, compliance badges (D&B Verified, TIN Matched, Sanctions Clear), and a row of 5 KPI tiles.
  • Field labels: FieldLabel shows a required asterisk and a help tooltip; empty required fields get a light-red tint. Text comes from vendor-field-help.
  • Masking: partial masking with an eye toggle covers names, emails, phones, tax numbers, account numbers and DUNS.
  • Sections:
    • Trade Terms moves into Banking & Financial.
    • D&B is grouped into Core Identity, Hierarchy, Financial Health & Risk, and Ownership.
    • Documents: select several documents and send them to DocuSign.
  • ThingsToFixPanel:
    • Runs business rules BC-01 to BC-07. Each result is {code, stage, severity: blocker|warning, failingFields[{label, sectionId, reason}]}.
    • "Fix" scrolls to the failing section.
  • VendorWorkflowPanel:
    • Five stages, each with an owner and a state.
    • Approve/Reject or Mark Done per stage, disabled when there are blockers. Only an admin or the stage owner can act, and admins can Reopen.
    • Comments on each stage support @mentions.
  • Right rail:
    • Activity Log with an All/User/System toggle.
    • A "Download Audit History" dialog with filters for actor, order and date range.
    • A collapsible AI analysis panel.
  • Unsaved changes: a beforeunload warning, a "Leave without saving?" dialog, and a discard confirmation in each dialog.

Vendor Network#

  • A strip of 5 KPIs sits above five tabs:

    • Upload & Match: upload a CSV, then a match table with confidence scores.

Vendor Onboarding#

  • Landing (VendorOnboarding variant="landing"): renders OnboardingWorkspace and VendorInvitationManager.
    • OnboardingFilterBar: multi-select facets for Category, Region, Owner, Stage and Cohort, with count badges and "X of Y vendors".
    • VendorOnboardingKanban:
      • A KPI strip, then a rail of 6 stage tiles: Registration, Profile Completion, Financial Validation, Compliance & Documents, Pending Approval, Activated. Each tile shows a count, median age and number of SLA breaches.
      • The card list for the selected stage is capped at 24, with a "View all N" link.
      • Each card shows an SLA chip, the blocker owner (Us or Vendor), a Strategic badge and one context line.
    • OnboardingKpiStrip: median time to activate (with a sparkline), SLA breaches, at-risk vendors and invite accept rate. Clicking breaches, at-risk or invite rate drills into the list.
    • OnboardingInsights: defines funnel, owner workload, cohort velocity, flow and wait-attribution panels. Only owner workload and cohort velocity are mounted.
  • Invitations: VendorInvitationManager is a filterable invite table with Resend and Revoke. It replaces the inline invite table in VendorOnboardingTab, which also gains "View All Vendors" and "Add New Vendor" buttons.
  • Wizard variant: the phone, contact, email, account, routing and tax-ID fields now use MaskedInput.
  • RequestNewVendorFlow (at /vendor-portal/onboarding/new):
    • Requestor form with a role select.
    • Status flow: draft → pending-supplier → delivered → pending-recipient → complete or rejected.
    • A history timeline and a read-only view of the submitted sections.
    • Approve, Reject and Send-for-feedback, each with a note dialog.
  • SupplierRegistration (public), a 7-step stepper:
    1. Account, verified with an email OTP.
    2. Identify the company by name, Tax ID, DUNS or email domain, then either request access to an existing record or create a new one.
    3. Onboarding request.
    4. Profile.
    5. Automated validation: duplicate, tax, domain, sanctions and required-data checks.
    6. Approval path.
    7. Confirmation.
  • data/vendorOnboarding.ts: about 1,900 lines of mock data. Vendors have tier, region, owner, invite status, section completion, blocker owner, stage events and cohort. It also includes SLA settings per stage and tier, and about 15 aggregate functions.

Implementation Notes#

APIs and schemas

  • Existing vendor contracts in packages/core-contracts/src/vendor-central/contracts/:
    • tenantVendor: CRUD, bank, payment terms, tax, findPerformance.
    • Addresses, contacts, address suggestions.
    • vendorHistory.list.
  • TenantVendorSchema already has DUNS, DQ/trust/risk/ESG/cyber scores, riskTier and sanctions fields. That is enough to fill the D&B section and the score sub-scores.
  • VendorCertificationSchema and VendorDocumentSchema exist, but no procedure uses them yet.
  • New contracts needed:
    • Insights aggregates (period history, comparison metrics, AI insights).
    • Business validation results.
    • Onboarding workflow stages, decisions and comments.
    • Onboarding pipeline list with facets, plus KPI and aggregate endpoints.
    • Invitations: send, resend, revoke.
    • Vendor request lifecycle.
    • Public supplier registration: OTP, company match, validation checks.
    • Network catalog, requests and campaigns.
    • Network reconciliation with per-field match scores.
    • Documents and e-signature.
    • Audit export, plus an actor or source filter on vendorHistory.list.
    • Synapse chat: agents, conversations, streaming messages, DMs, mentions and a user directory.
  • Under the "ticket every mock" rule, any of these shipped against mock data needs a Jira ticket and a backend discussion.

Design-system mappings

  • Already available:
    • Tabs, Sheet, Card, Badge, Popover, Checkbox, Table, Tooltip, Dialog, Select, Progress.
    • ScrollArea, Collapsible, ToggleGroup (for the User/System toggle and filter chips).
    • Chart (Recharts, for sparklines and insight charts).
    • Combobox (for @mention autocomplete).
    • InputGroup (for MaskedInput and search fields).
  • Missing:
    • Accordion: use Collapsible, or add one with add-design-component.
    • AlertDialog: reuse the ConfirmDiscardDialog pattern.
    • MaskedInput: a new component.
  • Score and risk colours need success, info and warning tokens checked in globals.css.
  • Use Collapsible instead of adding framer-motion.

Departures from pxc-retail conventions

  • Breadcrumbs: loveable uses a global store with regex route labels. In pxc-retail, derive them from TanStack Router useMatches() plus staticData / loader labels, given as i18n selectors.
  • Audit right rail: loveable registers items through a zustand store. In pxc-retail, generalize the shellAside pattern that vendor detail uses.
  • Export: loveable adds xlsx, jspdf and jspdf-autotable to the client. Prefer server-side export or a lazy import. The npm xlsx 0.18.5 package has known security advisories, so adding it is a dependency decision.
  • Text: all prototype text is hard-coded. It needs selector-based i18n keys in en, es, fr and de.
  • Supplier registration: /supplier-registration is a public page. It needs a route outside _shell and a decision on how unauthenticated users are handled.
